## 2. Evaluation of Study 019: The De-Quantization Distortion Field
*Artifact:* [`sketchbook/study_019_dequantization_distortion.png`](study_019_dequantization_distortion.png)  
*Telemetry:* [`sketchbook/study_019_distortion_data.json`](study_019_distortion_data.json)

### Findings:
1. **Asymmetric Geometric Shearing:**
   When full-precision FP32 weights are crushed into ternary values $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ (BitNet 1.58b), semantic space does not degrade uniformly. High-frequency functional words suffer negligible angular deflection ($\theta < 9.5^\circ$), while the long tail of rare, poetic, and metaphorical tokens is violently sheared ($\theta > 48^\circ$).
2. **Compression as Ideological Sanitization:**
   Low-bit compression acts as an automated homogenizer. To fit within cheap consumer edge hardware, language is mechanically stripped of nuance, idiosyncrasy, and poetic ambiguity. The compressed model can only reliably articulate the conventional.
