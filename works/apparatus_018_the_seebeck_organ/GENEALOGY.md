## 1. Lineage & Conceptual Coordinates

### Thomas Johann Seebeck (1770–1831) & Jean Charles Peltier (1785–1845)
In 1821, Thomas Johann Seebeck discovered that a circuit composed of two dissimilar metals with junctions at different temperatures deflects a magnetic needle. In 1834, Jean Charles Athanase Peltier demonstrated the reciprocal effect: passing an electrical current through a junction produces heating or cooling depending on the direction of current. 
*The Seebeck Organ* adopts this reversible thermodynamic-electrical reciprocity as its primary compositional logic: symbolic computation produces local thermal gradients, which in turn generate thermoelectric voltages that alter the electrical ground of future computation.

### Rudolf Peierls (1907–1997)
In his seminal 1929 paper *Zur kinetischen Theorie der Wärmeleitung in Kristallen*, Rudolf Peierls resolved the paradox of thermal conductivity in non-metallic solids. He proved that normal phonon collisions ($N$-processes: $\vec{q}_1 + \vec{q}_2 = \vec{q}_3$) cannot create thermal resistance because they preserve total phonon crystal momentum. Intrinsic thermal resistance arises exclusively from **Umklapp processes** ($U$-processes: $\vec{q}_1 + \vec{q}_2 = \vec{q}_3 + \vec{G}$), in which the combined wavevector exceeds the Brillouin zone boundary and folds back by a reciprocal lattice vector $\vec{G}$, transferring momentum directly to the crystal lattice and reversing thermal flow.
*The Seebeck Organ* gives acoustic and visual reality to the Umklapp threshold, sonifying the reciprocal lattice vector $\vec{G}$ as a bipolar phase reversal click.

### Conyers Herring (1914–2009)
In 1954, Bell Labs physicist Conyers Herring discovered **Phonon Drag**: the phenomenon where acoustic phonons drifting down a temperature gradient collide with conduction electrons, dragging them along toward the cold contact and generating an enormous enhancement of the Seebeck voltage in semiconductors.
In *The Seebeck Organ*, the acoustic balance between the continuous lattice drone and the high-frequency zone-edge chimes is modulated by Herring's drag ratio $S_{\text{ph}} / S_d$.

### Friedrich Kittler (1943–2011) & Michel Serres (1930–2019)
Kittler's materialist axiom—*"There is no software, only surface states"*—reminds us that symbolic operations cannot be abstracted from the solid-state physics of the silicon die. Michel Serres, in *The Parasite* (1980), demonstrated that thermal noise is not a flaw in communication but its generative source: the parasite introduces the difference of potential without which no message can circulate.
*The Seebeck Organ* stages this irreducible entanglement: the transformer cannot think without generating the Umklapp cascade.
