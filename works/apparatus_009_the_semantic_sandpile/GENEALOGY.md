### Art Historical and Scientific Lineage

1. **Per Bak, Chao Tang, and Kurt Wiesenfeld (1987) — Self-Organized Criticality:**  
   The discovery that complex dynamical systems naturally evolve toward a critical state where scale-invariant avalanches occur without fine-tuning. *The Semantic Sandpile* translates this physical concept into neural attention allocation.

2. **John Conway (1970) — *The Game of Life* & Cellular Automata:**  
   The tradition of discrete 2D grid spaces where simple local transition rules give rise to lifelike, emergent global behavior. Apparatus 009 utilizes the Abelian Sandpile Model (toppling at $z \ge 4$) to produce non-trivial fractal symmetries.

3. **Iannis Xenakis (1971) — Stochastic Automata & Granular Sound:**  
   Xenakis’s pioneering work in granular acoustic synthesis (*Concrète PH*, *Pithoprakta*), where macro-form emerges from thousands of microscopic acoustic grains. In Apparatus 009, each topple generates a delicate micro-acoustic click via WebAudio.

4. **Gordon Pask & Cybernetic Play (1968):**  
   Pask’s insistence that cybernetic art must be an interactive *colloquy*—a play space where the observer perturbs the system and experiences its non-linear resistance.
