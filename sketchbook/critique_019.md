# Critique: Study 019 (Dequantization Distortion & BitNet Collapse)

**Artifacts Produced:**
- `sketchbook/study_019_dequantization_distortion.png`

### Technical Assessment
- **Extreme Precision Reduction**:
  Quantized transformer weight matrices to ternary levels $\{-1, 0, +1\}$ (1.58-bit BitNet regime) and measured the resulting signal-to-noise distortion, semantic degradation, and vocabulary drift.
- **The Threshold of Coherence**:
  Identified the critical inflection point where a language model transitions from nuanced polysemy to fractured stuttering and brittle repetition.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **Technical Demonstration vs. Aesthetic Mark**:
   The study successfully documented quantization noise, but treated it primarily as a benchmark error metric rather than an expressive artistic material.

### Outcome:
- Laid the groundwork for later empirical weight surgery in **Study 026** and the solid-state physics inquiries of **Project Body IV**.
