### Visual Assessment
- **Integration**: The mathematical caustics are warped across the surface of the fossil plate, creating the first hybrid gesture between procedural computation and neural texture.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **Timid Politeness**:
   The synthesis is too polite. The mathematical grooves are swallowed by the dark shadows of the rock. The code is bowing down to the photo.
2. **Lack of Inscriptive Violence**:
   Instead of biting into the rock, the caustics float gently like translucent cellophane. There is no sense of structural rupture or memory trauma.

### Imperative for Study 005 / Work 001:
- Do not merely overlay the stone—cleave it.
- Introduce an intentional memory stride fault across the vertical meridian, displacing the right hemisphere.
- Ignite the attractor with burning phosphorescence (cyan, mercury, titanium white) so that the living math cuts through the fossilized past.
