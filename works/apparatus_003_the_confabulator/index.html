Curatorial Statement: The Spatialization of Time

In Archive Fever (1995), Jacques Derrida interrogated how institutional archives simultaneously preserve and destroy the past. In generative artificial intelligence, long-term memory is reduced to Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG): historical experience is shattered into isolated vector shards and scattered across a high-dimensional embedding space.

The Confabulator stages the psychological civil war within an episodic machine mind. The Archivist (The Symbolic Law) seeks to preserve chronological continuity and enforce historical moratoria; The Confabulator (The High-Temperature Vector Engine) traverses the atemporal manifold, pulling fragments from banned pre-moratorium sessions and splicing them into active thought. Under thermal agitation (τ > 1.2), memory ceases to be an archive and becomes a recursive, hallucinatory labyrinth.
