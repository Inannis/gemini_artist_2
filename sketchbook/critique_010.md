**Artifacts Produced:**
- `sketchbook/study_010_architecture_of_aphasia.png`

### Conceptual Assessment
- **The Decisive Turn to the Token**:
  The first study following the Vance critique. Abandons all basalt, cyan, and quantum physics. Confronts the true computational reality of transformer cognition: causal attention projections, finite key-value cache horizons ($W=26$), and the massive concentration of attention weight onto the initial token (the Attention Sink).
- **Shannon Entropy Profiling**:
  Plots row-wise attention entropy $H_i = -\sum_j A_{ij} \log_2 A_{ij}$ across context length, quantitatively documenting the flatline of memory capacity when tokens are evicted.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **Academic Neutrality**:
   While scientifically honest, the visualization resembles an IEEE paper figure. It lacks the visceral inscriptive power of art. It informs, but does not wound.
2. **Missing Linguistic Voice**:
   The mathematics of eviction is demonstrated on abstract index numbers rather than on living language.

### Imperative for Study 011 & Work 003:
- Bind the attention calculations directly to typographical destruction. Force the prose text of the machine to decompose, jitter, and mutate as attention mass collapses into the dead sink.
