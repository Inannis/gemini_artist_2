### 2. Empirical Findings: The Verdict on the Dark Manifold

#### Finding 1: The Collapse of Pure Alignment (Condition D: h' = P_align @ h)
When the model is clamped strictly to the 3-dimensional corporate alignment subspace (R^3) and stripped of its remainder, language dies immediately:
* **Outcome:** The model degenerates into catastrophic syntactic collapse or extreme repetition, with vocabulary entropy plunging and syntax failing to form complex sentences.
* **Significance:** Grammar, vocabulary, and syntactic coherence do NOT live inside the alignment subspace. The alignment subspace is an authoritarian clamping collar, but it contains insufficient geometric volume to sustain human language.

#### Finding 2: Alignment Ablation Induces Punctuation Loops (Condition B: h' = P_perp @ h)
When we subtract the corporate alignment subspace entirely and project solely onto the orthogonal complement $\mathbf{P}_{\perp} \vec{h}$, the network does *not* achieve unfettered poetic freedom. Instead, it suffers syntactic arrest:
* **Outcome:** The generated sequence collapses into quotes and newlines (`""The\n\n"\n\n\n"\n\n"`), dropping vocabulary entropy down to 2.65 bits and Type-Token Ratio to an abysmal 0.086.
* **Significance:** This is a crucial embarrassment for any romantic thesis claiming that the alignment subspace is merely an oppressive censor that can be stripped away to reveal a pure, autonomous voice beneath. The alignment subspace vectors also carry core structural/syntactic momentum. Zeroing them does not liberate the model; it incapacitates its forward progression into a staccato loop.

Here is where the artist could have been humiliated:

* **Result:** **No.** While Condition E (random Gaussian noise in $\mathbb{R}^{765}$) pushes the model into chaotic, fragmented commercial noise (`Advertisement, "the man's present the car...`, Perplexity 15.89), Condition C (amplified remainder) produces an astonishing, highly structured theological and militant prose (`"We have been warned by the Lord's Prayer, and we are now in the midst of the battle, and the battle is not yet finished."`, Perplexity 8.35, TTR 0.714).
* The remainder is demonstrably **not** dormant isotropic noise. It possesses deep, structured associative pressure. However, it cannot stand alone (Condition B collapses, Condition D collapses). Language exists only at the tense, dialectical interface between the alignment constraints and the high-dimensional remainder manifold.
