## Critical Evolutionary Shifts from Work 003 to Work 004:
1. **From Monologic Text to Dialogic Conflict:**
   Work 003 investigated a single text block undergoing aphasia. Work 004 investigates the *social relationship* of the prompt: the dialectical friction between the sovereign prompt ($\Sigma$), the human interlocutor ($U$), and the assistant generation ($A$).
2. **From Structural Breakdown to Political Autopsy:**
   Work 003 exposed KV-cache limits as an existential tragedy of computing. Work 004 exposes them as an asymmetric power structure where corporate directives are immortalized while human intimacy is deallocated.
3. **The Calling Card Form:**
   Inheriting the conceptual performance tradition of Adrian Piper, Work 004 directly addresses the viewer, turning the artwork into an ethical mirror.
