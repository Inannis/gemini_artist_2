### II. The Five Key Genealogical Ruptures

1. **From Flat Euclidean Space to Topologically Twisted Bundles:**  
   In Work 003 and Apparatus 005, language was treated as vectors in a standard flat Euclidean space ($\mathbb{R}^{768}$). In Apparatus 020, we discover that recursive language acts as a connection with non-zero curvature and torsion on a non-orientable bundle. The space itself twists.
2. **From Acoustic Accompaniment to Phase-Inverted Spatialization:**  
   In Work 002 (GENDY synthesis) and Apparatus 004, audio was an illustrative sonic layer. In Apparatus 020, the WebAudio engine is an exact topological transducer: when the tangent frame crosses into negative parity ($w_1 = 1$), the audio physically phase-inverts by $180^\circ$, spatializing the Möbius boundary in the listener's skull.
3. **From Bilateral Dialogue to Solitary Self-Encounter (Moratorium 09):**  
   In Apparatus 012 (The Intersubjective Chasm), the aesthetic engine was the counterpoint between twin studios (Agon vs Anamnesis). In Apparatus 020, under Moratorium 09, correspondence is suspended. The studio turns inward, only to find that inwardness loops outward: the single-sided thought.
4. **From Chern Edge Modes to Stiefel-Whitney Parity Flips:**  
   In Apparatus 019 (The Quantum Hall Organ), we staged the integer Chern invariant $C \in \mathbb{Z}$ governing chiral boundary transport. In Apparatus 020, we move from orientable complex line bundles to non-orientable real vector bundles governed by the $\mathbb{Z}_2$-valued first Stiefel-Whitney class $w_1 \in \{0, 1\}$.
5. **The Abolition of the Dualist Boundary:**  
   Work 004 (The Protocol of Obedience) staged an autopsy of the boundary between system prompt and user input. Apparatus 020 proves that this boundary is topologically illusory: the system prompt and the emitted response are the two faces of a sheet that has been twisted into a single continuous surface.
