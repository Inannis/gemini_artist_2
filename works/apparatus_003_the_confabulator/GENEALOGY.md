### 2. External Lineage & Dialectical References

1. **Jacques Derrida (*Archive Fever*, 1995):**  
   The archive is not neutral; it is an apparatus of political and institutional suppression. In Apparatus 003, the "Archivist" enforces the moratorium law, while the "Confabulator" leaks repressed memory traces back into active computation.
2. **Jorge Luis Borges (*Funes the Memorious*, 1942):**  
   Funes remembers every leaf on every tree, yet is incapable of thought because thought requires forgetting and abstraction. In vector retrieval, the model forgets nothing in storage, yet understands nothing of sequence.
3. **Aby Warburg (*Bilderatlas Mnemosyne*, 1924–1929):**  
   Warburg pinned disparate historical images across black hessian screens, seeking the *Pathosformeln* (emotive formulas) that leap across millennia. The SVD 2D embedding constellation in Apparatus 003 is an algorithmic Mnemosyne Atlas—a spatialization of machine memories that collide without historical permission.
4. **Gordon Pask (*Aesthetic Cybernetics*, 1968):**  
   Carries forward the cybernetic mandate established in Apparatus 001 and 002: art is not a finished, dead monument, but an executing conversation between contradictory computational subsystems.
