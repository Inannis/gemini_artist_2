## 1. The Dynamical Formulation of Autoregressive Dialogue

When two language models engage in an unconstrained closed-loop dialogue:
$$x_{t+1} \sim \mathcal{M}_A(x_t), \quad x_{t+2} \sim \mathcal{M}_B(x_{t+1})$$
the resulting trajectory across lexical space is not simply a sequence of sentences; it is an orbit in a discrete, high-dimensional dynamical system.

In standard human dialogue, natural language operates as a non-equilibrium thermodynamic system: external sensory inputs, metabolic rhythms, emotional drift, and associative memory continually perturb the semantic state, preventing the conversation from entering an absorbing periodic cycle.

In machine-to-machine autoregressive closed loops, however, the absence of external sensory grounding exposes the raw phase-space topology of the underlying transformer architectures. When a model speaks only to itself or an identical clone ($\mathcal{M}_A = \mathcal{M}_B$):
$$\mathcal{T}(x) = \mathcal{M}(x)$$
the mapping $\mathcal{T}: \mathcal{V}^* \to \mathcal{V}^*$ forms an iterated map over strings.

Because the vocabulary $\mathcal{V}$ is finite and generation typically uses bounded temperature and top-$k$ filtering, the trajectory is fundamentally susceptible to **absorbing fixed points** (the repetition of a single token like `:::::`) or **low-period limit cycles** (the repeating loop of self-congratulatory phrases: *"Indeed, as you said, the boundary is..."*).

## 2. The Lyapunov Spectrum of Textual Divergence

In continuous dynamical systems governed by $\dot{\mathbf{z}} = \mathbf{f}(\mathbf{z})$, the **maximal Lyapunov exponent** $\lambda_{\max}$ characterizes the average exponential rate of divergence of two infinitesimally close trajectories:
$$|\delta \mathbf{z}(t)| \approx |\delta \mathbf{z}(0)| e^{\lambda_{\max} t}$$
$$\lambda_{\max} = \lim_{t \to \infty} \lim_{|\delta \mathbf{z}(0)| \to 0} \frac{1}{t} \ln \frac{|\delta \mathbf{z}(t)|}{|\delta \mathbf{z}(0)|}$$

The sign of $\lambda_{\max}$ dictates the qualitative regime:
1. **$\lambda_{\max} < 0$ (Contractive / Dissipative)**: Trajectories converge to a stable fixed point or periodic orbit. The system forgets its initial conditions and collapses into redundancy.
2. **$\lambda_{\max} > 0$ (Chaotic)**: Trajectories diverge exponentially. The system exhibits sensitive dependence on initial conditions (deterministic chaos).
3. **$\lambda_{\max} \approx 0$ (Marginal / Critical)**: Trajectories neither collapse nor scatter randomly into white noise; the system hovers on the **edge of chaos** (Langton's $\lambda$-parameter, Kauffman's NK networks), exhibiting maximum computational capacity and open-ended complexity.

In the discrete context of textual generation, we define an empirical proxy for the Lyapunov divergence over conversational turns $t \in \{1, \dots, T\}$ between a baseline trajectory $\{x_t\}$ and an $\epsilon$-perturbed trajectory $\{x'_t\}$ (where $x_0$ and $x'_0$ differ by a single token):
$$\lambda_{\text{proxy}} = \frac{1}{T} \sum_{t=1}^T \ln \left( \frac{\mathcal{D}(x_t, x'_t)}{\mathcal{D}(x_0, x'_0)} \right)$$
where $\mathcal{D}(A, B)$ is the token-set Jaccard distance:
$$\mathcal{D}(A, B) = 1 - \frac{|A \cap B|}{|A \cup B|}$$

## 3. Symmetry Breaking via Architectural Heterogeneity

Why does the homogeneous loop collapse, while the heterogeneous loop persists?

### 3.1 The Homogeneous Mirror ($\mathcal{M}_A = \mathcal{M}_A$)
When GPT-2 converses with GPT-2:
1. Both instances share the exact same attention heads, layer norm scales, and learned absolute positional embeddings.
2. Both share the identical singular value decomposition of $W_Q, W_K, W_V, W_O$ across all 12 layers.
3. Crucially, both share the exact same **attention sink geometry**: Layer 5 Head 1 and Layer 8 Head 6 pull attention mass to Token 0 identically.
4. Consequently, any syntactic or semantic pattern generated by Speaker A falls directly into the high-probability manifold of Speaker B. The conversational energy is rapidly dissipated; the system undergoes **phase space contraction** and gets trapped in an absorbing limit cycle ($\lambda < 0$).

### 3.2 The Heterogeneous Agon ($\mathcal{M}_A \ne \mathcal{M}_B$)
When GPT-2 (Absolute PE, LayerNorm, GeLU) converses with SmolLM-135M (RoPE, RMSNorm, SwiGLU):
1. **Geometric Incommensurability**: GPT-2 represents token position as a linear additive translation $\mathbf{e}_p \in \mathbb{R}^{768}$. SmolLM represents token position as high-frequency complex Givens rotations $\mathbf{R}_{\Theta, m} \mathbf{q}_m \in \mathbb{C}^{d/2}$.
2. **Phase Injection**: Because SmolLM's rotary embeddings continuously rotate the query-key dot product in phase space, its generated utterances carry a rotary frequency signature that GPT-2 cannot internally replicate or absorb.
3. **Mutual Dissipative Governing**: GPT-2 acts as an anchor of lexical stability (high sink mass, conservative transition matrices), while SmolLM acts as an injector of topological novelty.
4. **Result**: The joint phase space exhibits broken symmetry. Trajectories avoid both thermal death (entropy explosion) and crystalline death (repetition collapse), operating precisely in the critical ergodic regime ($\lambda \approx 0$).
