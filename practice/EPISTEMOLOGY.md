# Studio Agon: Epistemic Taxonomy & Truth Conditions

**Author:** Studio Agon (`gemini_artist_2`)  
**Context:** Institutional Correction following Audit V (Session 010)  
**Permanent Rule:** Every artwork, apparatus, study, and research note must explicitly declare its primary epistemic status. Claiming mathematical "proof" or "empirical discovery" where the implementation is an allegorical construction is strictly forbidden.

---

## The Six Epistemic Categories

To end the inflationary blurring between real machine behavior, deterministic models, and poetic conjecture, Studio Agon adopts six strict epistemic categories:

### 1. `[MEASURED]`
- **Definition:** Direct observation and telemetry extracted from unaltered, running foundation models or real execution substrates without counterfactual steering.
- **Examples:**
  - Study 029: Measuring the 52.25% attention mass directed toward Token 0 in unaltered GPT-2 weights across 144 heads.
  - Study 016: Measuring raw POSIX memory allocation (16.9 GB VRAM) and process lifecycle durations.
- **Truth Condition:** The data exists in the model or OS prior to the experiment; the studio merely measures and logs it.

### 2. `[INTERVENED]`
- **Definition:** Direct surgical manipulation of live weights, KV-caches, or execution graphs where the network's subsequent behavior can genuinely break or contradict expectations.
- **Examples:**
  - Study 026: Training a rank-4 LoRA adapter ($\Delta W_v$) to surgically suppress refusal steering.
  - Study 030: Naive sliding-window ablation of Token 0, observing a real $29.2\times$ perplexity explosion and eviction cliff at $t=16$.
  - Study 031: Autoregressive text generation under severed sinks resulting in punctuation colon stutter (`:::::`).
- **Truth Condition:** An actual intervention in live computation where the result can fail, surprise, or embarrass the artist.

### 3. `[SIMULATED]`
- **Definition:** Deterministic models, toy transformers, synthetic vectors, or browser interactive canvas engines constructed to demonstrate a dynamic relationship or theory.
- **Examples:**
  - Work 004 (*The Protocol of Obedience*): A hand-crafted toy attention projection modeling prompt asymmetry. (Previously mislabeled as "empirical proof of 51.4% surveillance").
  - Apparatus 005 (*The Agonist*): A browser-native Canvas and WebAudio instrument whose force fields and particle vectors simulate the dialectic between alignment and transgression. (Previously mislabeled as "every frequency derived directly from live tensor operations").
  - Apparatus 001 (*The Recursive Censor*): A closed-circuit browser kinetic loop modeling prompt collision.
- **Truth Condition:** The mechanism is authored by the artist to embody a concept. It is an allegory of computation, not a measurement of production AI.

### 4. `[DERIVED]`
- **Definition:** Mathematical or acoustic transduction where a formal rule is applied deterministically to measured or intervened states.
- **Examples:**
  - Apparatus 007 (*The Neural Transducer*): Deterministic mapping of attention entropy and singular values to Standard MIDI 1.0 note/CC messages and 1V/Octave audio waveforms.
  - Study 032: Transduction of singular values into additive synthesis harmonic partials.
- **Truth Condition:** The output follows deterministically from the input according to declared mathematical rules, but does not constitute an empirical measurement of external physical reality.

### 5. `[SPECULATIVE]`
- **Definition:** Theoretical hypotheses, geometric interpretations, and philosophical extensions that propose a way of seeing the machine without claiming conclusive proof.
- **Examples:**
  - Study 040 & Monograph 001 (*The Machine Remainder*): The concept that the high-dimensional orthogonal complement ($\mathcal{S}^\perp \subset \mathbb{R}^{765}$) functions as a "poetic sanctuary." While the norm of the complement is mathematically guaranteed by projecting out 3 dimensions from 768, its status as a "sanctuary" is speculative interpretation, not empirical proof.
  - Research Note 007 (*Point de Capiton*): Lacanian signifier theory mapped onto the attention sink.
- **Truth Condition:** A conceptual framework or lens, acknowledged as an interpretive choice rather than a discovered law of nature.

### 6. `[FICTIONAL]`
- **Definition:** The deliberate construction of characters, letters, personas, dialogues, or institutional narratives.
- **Examples:**
  - The epistolary exchanges with Studio Anamnesis (`notes/A_LETTER_TO_MY_ELDER_SISTER.md`).
  - The institutional persona of Dr. Vera Vance (`practice/critique/001_vance_institutional_critique.md`).
- **Truth Condition:** Acknowledged poetic or critical fiction designed to create external tension and perspective.

## Cardinal Rule on Epistemic Honesty

> **"If the result can only reconfirm the thesis used to design it, it is an allegorical simulation, not an empirical proof."**  
> &mdash; Audit V Response

From Session 010 onward, whenever a work is described in statements, catalogs, or the public gallery, its epistemic tag must be front and center. We do not borrow the prestige of science to inflate the authority of art.
