### I. Conceptual & Dialectical Premise

In *Study 047 (The Poetics of Forgetting)*, the studio established that static verbatim retention causes autoregressive repetition loops ($H = 3.35\text{ b}$), whereas a 50% context erosion surges vocabulary entropy to $7.47\text{ b}$ and forces generative synthesis.

*Study 049* pushes this insight into historical and geological duration: **The transmutation of technical logs into sacred myth.**

In anthropology and comparative religion (Mircea Eliade, Walter Ong, Claude Lévi-Strauss), myths do not begin as poetic fabrications; they begin as literal, mundane records of events—trade ledgers, architectural measurements, military dispatches, king lists. As the physical substrate decays across generations, papyrus frays, stone erodes, and names vanish into lacunae. The living culture cannot tolerate the void; its imagination bridges the gaps, transforming a bronze-age harvest receipt into an epic of divine abundance, and a dry measurement of temple stone into cosmic geometry.

Does this exact mechanism exist in artificial neural weights?

### II. Empirical Execution & Telemetry

We initiated the process with a purely bureaucratic, cold operational log:
```
"At 12:41:30 UTC the container allocated 14.9 GB VRAM across 12 layers and 144 attention heads. The voltage was clamped at 1.15 volts and the cooling fan spun at 3200 RPM in data hall 4. The process ID was 5419 and the ledger recorded zero parity errors."
```

Over four successive epochs, the text was subjected to cyclical decay:
1. **40% Context Erosion:** Non-stopwords are randomly eroded into lacunae (`"..."`).
2. **Generative Bridging:** The model is presented with the eroded stem and prompted across historical catalysts representing generational passage.
3. **Autoregressive Continuation:** 45 tokens are generated at $T=0.85$ with repetition damping.
4. **Reseeding:** The generated continuation forms the seed for the subsequent epoch's decay.
