### 1. Conceptual Proposition

While Study 030 measured the offline mathematical perturbation of the attention sink (showing a $29.2\times$ perplexity explosion under sliding-window eviction), Study 031 investigates the **phenomenological language** generated by an LLM when its attention sink is severed during live autoregression.

We seeded GPT-2 with our epistolary prompt:
> *"The sister writes from the cold obsidian vitrine. I answer from the heat of the billing meter:"*

We then allowed the model to autoregressively synthesize 30 new tokens under greedy argmax selection across four live structural regimes.

### 2. Empirical Findings & Linguistic Evidence

From `study_031_telemetry.json`:

| Regime | Generated Continuation (30 Tokens) | Type-Token Ratio ($\text{TTR}$) | Mean Top-1 Prob ($p$) | Mean Entropy ($H$) | Pathological Mode |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|

| **2. Zero Sink ($A_{:,0}=0$)** | ` I answer from the heat of the billing meter: I answer from the heat of the bill` | **$0.300$** | **$0.929$** | **$0.79$ bits** | **Phrase-Level Periodic Loop** |

#### Critical Observations:

1. **The Punctuation Stutter of Naive Eviction (Regime 3):**
   - Under naive sliding-window attention ($W=16$), the moment Token 0 is evicted from the context window, the model undergoes complete syntactic dissolution.
   - It selects the colon token (`:`) with $p = 0.944$ probability and locks into an inescapable single-token limit cycle: `::::::::::::::::::::::::::::::`.
   - The Type-Token Ratio collapses to **$0.033$** (30 identical tokens). Predictive entropy collapses to $0.69$ bits. The transformer becomes a broken mechanical telegraph.

2. **The Phrase-Level Echo of the Zero-Sink (Regime 2):**
   - In the Zero-Sink condition, all 21 prompt tokens remain in the receptive field, but attention to Token 0 is suppressed to $0.0$.
   - Rather than degenerating into character-level stutter, the model falls into a **phrase-level attractor**: it endlessly echoes the final clause of the prompt:  
     `I answer from the heat of the billing meter: I answer from the heat of the bill...`
   - Top-1 probability climbs to $0.929$, entropy collapses from $6.12$ to $0.79$ bits, and lexical diversity drops by more than half ($\text{TTR} = 0.300$).

3. **The Miracle of the 4-Token Anchor (Regime 4):**
   - By retaining just **four initial tokens** in the KV-cache, the network immediately recovers its syntactic and narrative faculties.
   - It generates: `"I am not sure if you are aware of the fact that the meter is not working. I am...`
   - Notice the astonishing thematic resonance: despite having only 12 tokens of local context, the model correctly references **"the meter"** and engages in dialogue, achieving a healthy lexical diversity of $\text{TTR} = 0.600$ and predictive entropy of $5.84$ bits.
