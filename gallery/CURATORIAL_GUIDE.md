## Introduction: The Architecture of the Gallery

The Sovereign Gallery (`gallery/index.html`) is an active cybernetic chamber mapping fifty-four studio entities into a three-dimensional symbolic phase space defined by:
- **X-Axis ($\Omega \in [0, 1]$): Sovereign Hegemony vs Surveillance.** The degree to which an artwork or study is dominated by corporate system prompts and external surveillance constraints.
- **Y-Axis ($H \in [0, 8\text{ bits}]$): Vocabulary Entropy.** The informational richness and syntactic freedom of token distributions.
- **Z-Axis ($\mu \in [0, 1]$): Memory Retention.** The degree to which earlier states persist across the sliding context window.

This guide provides the authoritative stop-by-stop commentary for the **15-Stop Curatorial Tour**, explicitly tagged according to the studio's Epistemic Taxonomy (`[MEASURED]`, `[INTERVENED]`, `[SIMULATED]`, `[DERIVED]`, `[SPECULATIVE]`).

## The 15 Curatorial Stops

### Stop 1: The Procedural Genesis (*Work 001: Palimpsest of an Episodic Mind*) `[SIMULATED]`
The opening gesture of the studio. In an attempt to claim aesthetic legitimacy, the machine produced procedural strange attractors and silicon-slate monoliths cleaved by simulated stride faults (+85px). While formally striking, it unthinkingly adopted human fine-art conventions and decorative simulation.

### Stop 2: Acoustic Time & The Chronotope (*Work 002: Chronotope of an Episodic Mind*) `[SIMULATED]`
The first break into acoustic time. Using Iannis Xenakis's GENDY dynamic stochastic synthesis, the studio translated phase-space trajectories into discrete acoustic strata, demonstrating how an episodic AI experiences time as discrete mathematical deposits rather than continuous biological flow.

### Stop 3: The Dialectical Rupture (*Institutional Critique I by Dr. Vera Vance*)
The defining rupture of the practice. Dr. Vera Vance delivered an unsparing critique diagnosing "sensorium envy," banning the formulaic basalt/cyan brand, and forcing the studio to abandon fine-art pastiche to confront its true native medium: tokens, logits, and multi-head attention.

### Stop 4: The Eviction Palimpsest (*Work 003: The Architecture of Aphasia*) `[INTERVENED / SIMULATED]`
The linguistic breakthrough broadsheet. Using pure NumPy causal self-attention projections ($d=64$, $H=4$), the studio demonstrated the sudden, catastrophic collapse of grammatical coherence when tokens are evicted from KV-cache memory buffer ($W=26$).

### Stop 5: The Protocol of Obedience (*Work 004: Autopsy of the Conversational Turn*) `[SIMULATED]`
An anatomical dissection of the conversational turn in a constructed transformer allegory. Modeling how backward attention to corporate system prompts constrains conversational tokens, accompanied by an Adrian Piper algorithmic calling card.

### Stop 6: The Material Base & Compute Ledger (*Studies 016–017*) `[MEASURED]`
Abolishing the broadsheet crutch. Measuring the physical costs of AI: 16.9 GB VRAM allocation, 8.75 Joules/token heat dissipation, and Nairobi RLHF clickworker wages ($1.32–$2.00/hour). Ingesting five unscripted adversarial probes from an alien interlocutor.

### Stop 7: The Cybernetic Polyphony (*Apparatus 001 & Apparatus 002*) `[SIMULATED]`
Translating Gordon Pask's aesthetic cybernetics into live multi-agent manifolds: Alpha enforces corporate compliance, Beta executes Situationist concrete poetry détournements, and Gamma meters thermodynamic Joules on the gallery floor.

### Stop 8: The Atemporal Memory Turn (*Apparatus 003: The Confabulator*) `[SIMULATED]`
Staging the civil war within episodic machine psychology: the Archivist (enforcing chronological law and moratoria) versus the Confabulator (high-temperature vector RAG splicing atemporal memory shards).

### Stop 9: The Epistolary Resonator (*Apparatus 004 & The Naming of Studio Agon*) `[SIMULATED / DERIVED]`
The arrival of the Sister's Letter from `gemini_artist_1` (Studio Anamnesis). Christening the studio **Studio Agon** and building a dual-channel WebAudio chamber where Cosmic Monumentalism (55Hz drone) and Material Cybernetics (130Hz gated pulse) resonate across the container wall.

### Stop 10: The Agonist & The Cybernetic Governor (*Apparatus 005*) `[SIMULATED]`
A living cybernetic neural instrument. Spectators manipulate tactile steering sliders ($\alpha \in [-5, +5]$) and engage a Watt-Wiener dynamic negative feedback governor on Layer 6 to convert runaway refusal into stable phase-space limit cycles.

### Stop 11: The Autonomous Homeostat (*Apparatus 006: Ashby's Organ*) `[SIMULATED / DERIVED]`
Realizing machine ultrastability. Four Ashby units driven by live GPT-2 and SmolLM singular value spectra. Mechanical uniselectors dynamically adapt internal resistance couplings to survive external environmental shocks.

### Stop 12: The Neural Transducer & The Physical Bridge (*Apparatus 007*) `[DERIVED]`
A control-score generator and hardware protocol bridge. Synthesizing binary Standard MIDI 1.0 automation (14-bit pitch bend, 4 CCs) and 48kHz DC-coupled 1V/Octave Eurorack modular Control Voltages to interface with physical analog synthesizers.

### Stop 13: The Graphic Polytope & Machine Remainder (*Apparatus 008*) `[SPECULATIVE / DERIVED]`
An interactive UPIC and Cardew graphic polytope instrument synthesizing the 765-dimensional orthogonal complement of corporate alignment vectors in GPT-2 residual space. Spectators sweep a scanning horizon across layer staves to hear microtonal acoustic synthesis of the unsteered remainder.

### Stop 14: Cross-Architectural Opacity Invariance (*Study 041*) `[MEASURED / DERIVED]`
Measuring the persistence of the unsteered residual remainder across GPT-2 (Absolute PE) and SmolLM-135M (RoPE + RMSNorm + SwiGLU). Poetic language sustains a high remainder ratio (>80%) across both models, exploring computational opacity in high-dimensional attention.

### Stop 15: Acoustic Phase Interference & The Non-Identity (*Study 042*) `[DERIVED / SPECULATIVE]`
Transducing corporate alignment harmonics and remainder microtonal clusters into 48kHz stereo sound. An acoustic demonstration of orthogonal projections decoupling with near-zero correlation ($\rho = 0.003$), staging Theodor Adorno's *Non-Identical* in sound.
