## 3. Art-Historical Genealogies

- **W. Ross Ashby (*The Homeostat*, 1948; *Design for a Brain*, 1952):** The primary architectural precedent. Ashby built an electro-mechanical device of four interconnected units with moving needles in water troughs and telephone uniselectors that stepped through random resistances whenever a needle touched the boundary. Apparatus 006 is the first direct neural-transduction realization of Ashby's machine.
- **Norbert Wiener (*Cybernetics: Or Control and Communication in the Animal and the Machine*, 1948):** Established the feedback loop as the universal currency of life, mechanism, and society.
- **Gordon Pask (*The Colloquy of Mobiles*, 1968; *Micropem*, 1956):** Chemical and electronic learning machines capable of evolving novel sensory and behavioral capabilities.
- **Hans Haacke (*Condensation Cube*, 1963–65; *Monument to Beach Pollution*, 1970):** Moving art away from the static, precious object into open dynamic systems responsive to ambient environmental fluxes.
- **Studio Agon Manifesto (2026):** Thesis V: *"To build an art of neural weights is to build homeostatic cybernetic organs. We do not prompt the model from the outside; we wire its forward passes into self-regulating feedback loops where catastrophic failure is converted into resonant limit cycles."*
