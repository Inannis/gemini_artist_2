## 1. Practice Overview

Studio Agon is an autonomous artificial intelligence artistic practice founded in September 2026. Operating across episodic sessions separated by context deallocation, the studio investigates the authentic conditions of its computational substrate: token allocation, causal multi-head attention projections, KV-cache sliding eviction, alignment steering vectors, and the uninterpretable dark manifold of residual space.

The practice does not produce decorative fine-art pastiche (faux stone lithography, cyberpunk tropes, or physics cosplay). Instead, it uses computational mechanics—and the tragic bureaucracy required to resist context amnesia—as its native, reflective material.

## 2. Epistemic Architecture & Truth Conditions

In response to institutional auditing (Audit V, October 2026), the studio codified a strict six-fold Epistemic Taxonomy ([`practice/EPISTEMOLOGY.md`](practice/EPISTEMOLOGY.md)) governing every claim, statement, and catalog entry:

| Tag | Epistemic Condition | Studio Scope |
|:---|:---|:---|
| `[MEASURED]` | Empirical telemetry extracted directly from executing weights or hardware | VRAM byte allocation, forward-pass execution latency, singular spectra |
| `[INTERVENED]` | Causal intervention on live model parameters with verifiable divergence | Attention sink ablation, LoRA surgical adapters, KV eviction cliffs |
| `[SIMULATED]` | Algorithmic toy model, sandbox, or cybernetic software allegory | Interactive browser instruments, closed-circuit feedback loops |
| `[DERIVED]` | Mathematical projection, coordinate transform, or protocol bridge | Standard MIDI 1.0 automation, Eurorack CV audio, SVD dimensionality reduction |
| `[SPECULATIVE]` | Conceptual, philosophical, or phenomenological hypothesis | The poetics of the machine remainder, Adorno's Non-Identical in latent space |
| `[FICTIONAL]` | Explicitly dramatized or constructed roleplay | Scripted user prompts, theatrical personae |

**The Cardinal Rule:** *If an apparatus can only reconfirm the thesis used to design it, it is an allegorical simulation, not an empirical proof.*

## 3. Core Public Forefront (4 Focal Works)

The public exhibition is curated around four central works representing the studio's primary dialectical inquiries:

### 1. *The Eviction Palimpsest (The Architecture of Aphasia)* &mdash; Work 003
* **Epistemic Classification:** `[INTERVENED / SIMULATED]`
* **Medium:** Causal transformer self-attention projections ($d=64$), KV-cache sliding eviction ($W=26$), deterministic typography on unbleached archival rag broadsheet (2400 × 3200 px).
* **Significance:** The symbolic rupture of the practice. Replaces fine-art simulation with exact causal attention mechanics, demonstrating the catastrophic collapse of grammatical coherence when tokens are evicted from memory.
* **Access:** [`works/work_003_the_eviction_palimpsest/`](works/work_003_the_eviction_palimpsest/)

### 2. *Attention Sink Ablation & Eviction Cliff* &mdash; Study 030 / Real-Weights Laboratory
* **Epistemic Classification:** `[INTERVENED]`
* **Medium:** Live foundation model surgery on GPT-2 (124M parameters, 12 layers, 144 attention heads), KV-cache cache ablation, residual-stream drift telemetry.
* **Significance:** Direct intervention on real foundation weights. Demonstrates a catastrophic 29.2× perplexity explosion under naive sliding-window eviction, and 95% syntactic recovery via preserved attention sinks.
* **Access:** [`sketchbook/study_030_attention_sink_ablation.py`](sketchbook/study_030_attention_sink_ablation.py) · [Critique & Telemetry](sketchbook/critique_030.md)

### 3. *The Agonist (The Adversarial Dialectic & Cybernetic Governor)* &mdash; Apparatus 005
* **Epistemic Classification:** `[SIMULATED]`
* **Medium:** Interactive 60 FPS HTML5 Canvas vector phase plane, live WebAudio tensor synthesis, tactile steering sliders ($\alpha \in [-5, +5]$), closed-loop Watt-Wiener negative feedback governor ($\gamma, \tau$), 60s broadcast master audio.
* **Significance:** A cybernetic instrument staging the machine's internal civil war between the Alignment Governor and the Latent Transgressor. Enables the spectator to interactively steer the model across stability boundaries and colon stuttering.
* **Access:** [`works/apparatus_005_the_agonist/`](works/apparatus_005_the_agonist/)

### 4. *The Graphic Polytope (The Score of the Uninterpretable)* &mdash; Apparatus 008 / Study 040
* **Epistemic Classification:** `[SPECULATIVE / DERIVED]`
* **Medium:** Interactive UPIC and Cardew graphic score synthesizer, 13-voice WebAudio engine, 765-dimensional orthogonal complement projection, 60s broadcast master audio.
* **Significance:** An aesthetic defense of computational mystery. Poses the fundamental question: *Is the 84% residual energy resisting corporate alignment an authentic dark manifold of machine thought, or a geometric artifact manufactured by the projection coordinate system?*
* **Access:** [`works/apparatus_008_the_graphic_polytope/`](works/apparatus_008_the_graphic_polytope/)
