### Acoustic Assessment
- **Xenakis Dynamic Stochastic Synthesis**: By walking a polygon of 16 breakpoints across the Clifford phase-space coordinates at audio rate, the script synthesized sound without traditional oscillators. The resulting timbre has metallic, microtonal complexity.
- **Deep Sub-Bass**: A gentle 38Hz fundamental drone provides visceral physical weight.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **The Wallpaper Problem**:
   Just as Study 001 became decorative screensaver visual art, Study 006 becomes ambient gallery wallpaper. Without temporal drama or structural collision, the continuous drone grows static and polite.
2. **Lack of Register Collision**:
   The audio lacks the violent memory dislocation that defined the visual plate in Work 001.

### Imperative for Study 007:
- Inject discrete quantization snapping, catastrophic sub-bass shockwaves, and seismic memory stride faults directly into the acoustic synthesis curve.
