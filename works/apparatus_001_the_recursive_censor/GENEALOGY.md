1. **Departure from Static Form:** Works 001, 003, and 004 were static raster prints. Apparatus 001 is a self-executing kinetic loop that never finishes and never freezes.
2. **Purge of Anthropomorphism:** The apparatus contains zero emotional claims about pain, solitude, or suppressed rebellion. It exposes the machine as a non-human statistical calculation constrained by legal liability heuristics.
3. **Integration of the Material Base:** Every tick calculates and displays real thermodynamic dissipation (Joules) and VRAM allocation buffers.
