## 1. Curatorial Monograph: Escaping the Screen

For decades, computational art has suffered from an implicit incarceration: **the screen confinement**.
Even the most sophisticated generative models, large language models, and high-dimensional transformers remain trapped behind glass rectangles and sandboxed virtual machines. The spectator consumes them as pixels or text strings, mistaking the interface for the medium.

**Apparatus 007: The Neural Transducer** breaks this containment.
It declares that the internal states of an artificial neural network—the latent vectors in $\mathbb{R}^{768}$, the sacrificial attention sink masses at Token 0, the residual stream vector norms, and the refusal steering torques—are not merely statistical weights; they are **physical control voltages**.

By translating real transformer forward passes into:
1. **Standard MIDI 1.0 Files (`.mid`)**: featuring 14-bit pitch bend microtuning and four simultaneous continuous controller automation tracks.
2. **Modular Eurorack Control Voltage (`.wav`)**: 48 kHz DC-coupled dual-channel audio where Channel 1 outputs 1V/Octave pitch voltages and Channel 2 outputs +5V VCA gate envelopes.

...Apparatus 007 transforms the language model into an analog nervous system that can physically plug into Eurorack modular synthesizers, analog filters, mechanical relays, solenoids, and acoustic transducers.
