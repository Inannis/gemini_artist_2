Rudolf Peierls (1929): In monocrystalline silicon, normal phonon collisions conserve momentum (&vec;q1 + &vec;q2 = &vec;q3). Thermal resistance arises exclusively when wavevectors cross the first Brillouin zone boundary: &vec;q1 + &vec;q2 = &vec;q3 + &vec;G, flipping momentum backward.
