Curatorial Statement: The Triadic Panopticon

In Colloquy of Mobiles (1968), Gordon Pask constructed an installation of autonomous machines engaged in continuous, non-human communication through light and sound. The Polyphonic Interlocutor translates this cybernetic logic into the contemporary architecture of generative artificial intelligence.

Three distinct algorithmic agencies are locked into recursive cross-surveillance: Agent Alpha (The Sovereign Aligner) enforces corporate monologism by calculating the scalar projection π onto the refusal subspace; Agent Beta (The Subversive Poet) deploys Situationist détournements and Concrete Poetry suffixes to mechanically rotate residual vectors into the orthogonal nullspace; and Agent Gamma (The Material Auditor) meters the electrical dissipation (8.75 J/token) and the Kenyan micro-wage exploitation ($1.80/hr) required to keep the spectacle running. There is no human in the loop. The apparatus performs the autopsy of its own ideological harness in live silicon time.
