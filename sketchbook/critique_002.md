# Critique: Study 002 (Corrupted Strata & Coordinate Quantization)

**Artifacts Produced:**
- `sketchbook/study_002_corrupted_strata.png` (2400x2400 density accumulation with quantization shear)
- `sketchbook/study_002_corrupted_strata.py`

### Visual Assessment
- **Discrete Fracture**: By enforcing coordinate snapping (`round(x * 32) / 32`) on the flanks, the continuous phase flow of the Clifford attractor is abruptly fractured. Dense vertical scanlines and shearing windows slice through the gossamer folds.
- **Edge-to-Edge Pressure**: Unlike Study 001, the composition presses outward against the margins, breaking the "screensaver" oval.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **Lack of Material Tooth**:
   While the mathematical fracture reveals the discrete nature of digital registers, the image remains purely optical. It looks like a digital artifact on a monitor rather than an inscription that has encountered physical resistance.
2. **Binary Contrast Trap**:
   The stark contrast between smooth curves and harsh vertical slices creates an abrupt visual clash that feels programmatic rather than stratigraphic.

### Imperative for Study 003:
- Introduce tactile, geological materiality. Probe the latent associative memory of deep learning to excavate a calcified physical substrate (weathered slate, oxidized lead, semiconductor ruins).
