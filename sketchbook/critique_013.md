### Conceptual Assessment
- **Autopsy of the Conversational Turn**:
  Deconstructs the commercial chat interface by partitioning the prompt into three unequal sectors: $\Sigma$ (Sovereign Directive), $U$ (Interlocutor Query), and $A$ (Machine Generation).
- **The 51.4% Metric**:
  Demonstrates that over half of the generated token's attention mass is held hostage by the sovereign directive, proving that the conversational "dialogue" is structurally a three-way panopticon.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings
1. **Static Analytic Diagram**:
   Like Study 010, the visualization remains largely diagnostic. It proves the political asymmetry of the prompt, but needs to be weaponized into a provocative public encounter.
