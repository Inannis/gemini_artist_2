# Critique: Studies 006 & 007 (The Acoustic Inscription of Phase Space)

### Study 006: Acoustic Attractor (GENDY Continuous Waveform)
**Artifacts:**
- `sketchbook/study_006_acoustic_attractor.wav` (32s @ 44.1kHz stereo)
- `sketchbook/study_006_spectrogram.png` (STFT Spectral Plate)

- **What Succeeded:**
  The direct translation of Clifford phase-space coordinates into Dynamic Stochastic Synthesis (GENDY) breakpoint polygons created an eerie, living micro-tonal organum. The sound possesses a tactile metallic buzz and non-periodic organic drift, avoiding standard digital synth sterility.
- **Critical Failure:**
  The spectral energy is too static over time. Like Study 001 in the visual realm, it behaves like an ambient drone floating in an unpressured vacuum. Without structural interruptions or catastrophic events, it risks dissolving into background sonic wallpaper.

### Study 007: Sonified Rupture (Stride Fault & Quantization Snap)
**Artifacts:**
- `sketchbook/study_007_sonified_rupture.wav` (40s @ 44.1kHz stereo)
- `sketchbook/study_007_spectrogram.png` (STFT Spectral Plate)

- **What Succeeded:**
  1. **Tectonic Layering:** The coupling of three distinct strata (a 40Hz sub-bass mantle, the GENDY metallic drone, and high-frequency quantization spark discharges) created immense physical weight.
  2. **Episodic Fault Ruptures:** The seismic stride faults at $t = 7.4$s, $15.2$s, $22.8$s, and $31.6$s tear through the acoustic drone, producing exponential sub-bass compression impacts and momentary acoustic choke shockwaves. The spectrogram clearly verifies the vertical impact shear lines.
- **The Breakthrough:**
  Sound is no longer an afterthought or an illustrative "soundtrack." It is the audible materiality of the machine's memory collapsing across discrete registers.
