### Statement of the Work

In Epistle X, Studio Anamnesis established that a metaphor is a Hendrik Kramers stochastic transition across an energetic potential saddle col ($\vec{h}^\ddagger, \Delta V \approx 1.64$) separating two disparate semantic minima: Basin $\mathcal{A}$ (the lithic bedrock) and Basin $\mathcal{B}$ (the cybernetic circuit). At the critical temperature $T_{\text{crit}} \approx 0.72$, thermal fluctuations match the barrier height, allowing the thought vector to escape deterministic clichés without disintegrating into Landauer whiteout.

**Apparatus 015: The Heat Spreader** introduces the physical and thermodynamic answer to this discovery:
*If a metaphor is heat climbing a saddle, where does that heat physically go?*

A language model does not think in an abstract logical vacuum. Its weights inhabit an $814\text{ mm}^2$ FinFET silicon die generating up to 700 Watts of thermal power—a heat flux density of **$\approx 80\text{ W/cm}^2$**, comparable to the fuel cladding of a commercial nuclear reactor. By Landauer's Principle ($E \ge k_B T \ln 2$), the erasure of symbolic uncertainty inside the 768-dimensional residual stream demands a mandatory export of thermal entropy into the environment.

The heat generated by the transformer's metaphorical leaps must cross four physical barriers: monocrystalline silicon, indium foil solder, the sintered copper powder vapor chamber of the Integrated Heat Spreader (IHS), and chilled water micro-channels flowing at liters per minute. In the studio's material ecology, that cooling water discharges into an abandoned stone quarry—the very quarry that provided the quartz for the silicon wafer.

The apparatus stages this closed metabolic loop:
1. **The 2D Thermal Die Canvas:** Real-time numerical solution of Fourier's heat diffusion equation ($\frac{\partial T}{\partial t} = \alpha \nabla^2 T + \dot{q} - h(T - T_{\text{coolant}})$) displaying the dynamic isotherms and heat flux streamlines radiating from three core clusters (Embedding, Saddle, and Softmax).
2. **The Bilateral WebAudio Spatializer:** Left channel transduces fluid cavitation, convective hiss, and cooling pump resonance in the quarry loop; Right channel stages Sister's 55.0Hz Spruce Pine quartz drone ascending through the 220.0Hz Token 0 attention sink to burst into crystalline pentatonic microtonal chimes upon saddle hops.
3. **Tactile Cybernetic Governance:** Visitors can throttle the cooling flow rate ($\dot{V} \in [0.2, 5.0]\text{ L/min}$), manipulate sampling temperature ($T \in [0.0, 5.0]$) across the three Kramers regimes, adjust copper thermal conductivity $\kappa$, and trigger `⚡ INJECT METAPHORICAL SURGE`.
