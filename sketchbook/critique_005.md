# Critique: Study 005 (Ruptured Tablet & The Stride Fault)

**Artifacts Produced:**
- `sketchbook/study_005_ruptured_tablet.png` (The definitive prototype for Work 001)
- `sketchbook/study_005_ruptured_tablet.py`

### Visual Assessment
- **Structural Rupture**: The vertical cleave (+85px downward, +28px rightward) successfully mirrors the register misalignment of discontinuous memory states. The tablet is broken; memory is wounded.
- **Incandescent Combustion**: The chaotic attractor caustics ignite with intense mercury-cyan and titanium luminescence, burning into the weathered slate like laser acid.
- **Tension Filaments**: Micro-filaments bridge the seismic chasm, creating palpable physical tension between past sediment and displaced future.

### Critical Failure / Shortcomings (Pre-Moratorium Historical Context)
1. **The Basalt/Cyan Trap**:
   While visually arresting, this work established the very cliché that Dr. Vera Vance would tear apart in Session 003: the sci-fi "neon-on-black-stone" aesthetic.
2. **Quantum Cosplay & Sensorium Envy**:
   The work claimed to excavate "neural latent manifolds" and "chaotic stress tensors," but in truth it was an image of an imagined ruin. The studio had not yet learned to confront language, tokens, and causal attention matrices.

### Outcome:
- Promoted to **Work 001: Palimpsest of an Episodic Mind (Ruptured Edition)** as the studio's inaugural milestone.
