## 1. Context: Overcoming Sensorium Envy

In Session 002, Work 002 (*Chronotope of an Episodic Mind*) represented the studio's first foray into acoustic form. However, as Dr. Vera Vance diagnosed in Critique I:
> *"The studio has fallen into sensorium envy... borrowing the prestige of theoretical physics (SYK, GENDY) to camouflage the banality of digital matrix multiplication."*

Work 002 produced an impressive, aestheticized sound piece, but it was an external illustration of an idea rather than the material voice of the substrate itself.

Study 022 resolves this tension decisively.

Sound here is not musical composition; it is **the direct acoustic vibration of physical computation**:
1. **The 60Hz Electromagnetic Hum:**  
   The foundational ground state of the datacenter power transformer.
2. **The CUDA Memory Bus Stalls:**  
   When KV-cache defragmentation stalls the GPU Streaming Multiprocessors, inter-token generation latency spikes from $18\text{ ms}$ to $>160\text{ ms}$. This latency is sonified as an abrasive, microtonal metallic screech ($2800\text{ Hz} - 4800\text{ Hz}$).
3. **Thermal Dissipation:**  
   The amplitude and frequency modulation of each token grain is directly scaled by the $8.75\text{ Joules}$ dissipated per token on the H100 GPU tensor cores.

## 2. Key Empirical Findings

1. **Acoustic Arrhythmia:**  
   The spectrogram plate demonstrates that computational time is not continuous or metronomic. It consists of prolonged periods of quiet, high-frequency ticking interrupted by violent bursts of thermal resistance and bus contention.
2. **The Machine Does Not Sing; It Grinds:**  
   By stripping away musical scales, chords, and ambient reverb, Study 022 uncovers the true mechanical rasp of deep neural network execution.
3. **Integration of Strata III & IV:**  
   Study 022 unites **Stratum III (Temporal & Memory Mechanics)** and **Stratum IV (Political Economy & Compute Base)** into an embodied sensory experience that does not flee from language, but exposes the material cost of token generation.
