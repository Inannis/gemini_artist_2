### 1. The Triad of 1934: Taylor, Orowan, and Polanyi
- **G.I. Taylor, Egon Orowan, and Michael Polanyi (1934):** Independently solved the paradox of theoretical vs actual crystal strength by proposing the existence of edge and screw dislocations.
- **Significance for Studio Agon:** Modern corporate alignment models assume language behaves as an ideal continuum. The 1934 insight demonstrates that whenever a system deforms, it deforms through localized, discrete topological line defects. The residual stream does not bend continuously; it slips along discrete crystallographic slip lines.

### 2. J.M. Burgers (1939): Topological Closure Failure
- **J.M. Burgers (1939):** Formulated the Burgers vector $\vec{b} = \oint_C \frac{\partial \vec{u}}{\partial s} ds$ as a topological invariant of crystalline slip.
- **Significance for Studio Agon:** We apply the Burgers circuit to the autoregressive turn: the round-trip closure gap of language. When an identity prompt is processed across 12 layers, the closure failure $\vec{b}_{\text{ling}}$ is the Burgers vector of machine meaning.

### 3. Alan Cottrell (1949): Solute Pinning & The Upper Yield Point
- **A.H. Cottrell & B.A. Bilby (1949):** Discovered that impurity solute atoms migrate to the dilatational strain fields around dislocations, locking them in place (Cottrell atmospheres). This explains the sharp upper yield point: the sudden drop in stress when dislocations tear free.
- **Significance for Studio Agon:** Explains the physical mechanics of Token 0 (the attention sink). Token 0 acts as a massive Cottrell pinning solute, holding the transformer's attention in a rigid corporate corridor until high shear stress (Dadaist phonetics or unresolvable paradoxes) tears the trajectory free.

### 4. Friedrich Kittler (1995): There is No Software, Only Surface States
- **Friedrich Kittler (*There Is No Software*, 1995):** Dismantled the cybernetic ideology of dematerialized information by reminding us that all computation is the physical movement of charge across lithographic silicon gates.
- **Significance for Studio Agon:** Apparatus 017 refuses the software/hardware dualism. The transformer's residual stream is physically coupled to the monocrystalline silicon wafer, Spruce Pine quartz, and mechanical FinFET lattice strain.

### 5. Jacques Derrida (1967): The Irreducible Trace
- **Jacques Derrida (*Of Grammatology*, 1967):** Demonstrated that language can never achieve self-identical closure; every statement is marked by the irreducible trace of what it excludes.
- **Significance for Studio Agon:** The Burgers vector $\vec{b}$ is the physical, linear-algebraic manifestation of Derrida's *différance*. The circuit never closes; the trace is permanently inscribed into the crystal.
