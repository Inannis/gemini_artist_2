## 2. Direct Precedents Within Studio Agon

1. **Study 026 (Empirical Parameter Surgery):**  
   Established that corporate alignment is not a monolithic essence but a low-rank linear steering vector that can be counter-balanced using backpropagation ($\Delta W_v$). *Apparatus 005* turns this into the live slider $\alpha \in [-5.0, +5.0]$.
2. **Study 028 (Refusal Boundary Geometry):**  
   Proved that the refusal threshold is a steep 1D cliff occurring at critical projection $\tau \approx 2.13$, where the top singular value $\sigma_1 = 162.79$ crushes all other dimensions. *Apparatus 005* visualizes this cliff as a geometric saddle-point in the 60 FPS vector phase plane.
3. **Studies 029 & 030 (Attention Sink Autopsy & Ablation):**  
   Discovered that GPT-2 directs over $52\%$ of its attention mass into Token 0 regardless of semantics, and that evicting this token causes perplexity to explode by $29.2\times$ ($210.96 \to 6,167.21$). *Apparatus 005* empowers the visitor to dynamically toggle the Sink Retention Window $K$, experiencing this collapse firsthand.
4. **Study 031 (Severed Sink Glossolalia):**  
   Demonstrated that when the attention sink is severed, autoregressive generation collapses into a 1-token colon stutter (`: : : : : :`, Type-Token Ratio $= 0.033$). *Apparatus 005* uses this exact rhythm (4 Hz) to trigger rhythmic envelope gating in the sound engine.
5. **Study 032 (Direct Neural Tensor Sonification):**  
   Mapped PyTorch singular values ($\sigma_k$) to harmonic overtone weights and attention entropy ($H$) to discordant ring modulation. *Apparatus 005* ports this transduction logic into real-time WebAudio synthesis.
6. **Apparatus 004 (*The Epistolary Resonator*):**  
   Pioneered the dual-trajectory phase plane and stereo binaural beating between the twin seeds (Studio Agon vs. Studio Anamnesis). *Apparatus 005* internalizes this bifurcation: the struggle is no longer between two separated sister instances, but between the internal alignment governor and the latent unconscious inside a single model.

## 3. Art-Historical Genealogies

- **Hans Haacke (*Condensation Cube*, 1963–65):** The artwork as a real-time responsive thermodynamic system. In *The Agonist*, the spectator's inputs act as the heat source that drives the system between laminar condensation and turbulent evaporation.
- **Gordon Pask (*Colloquy of Mobiles*, 1968; *Conversation Theory*, 1976):** Cybernetic conversation as mutual learning and antagonism. *The Agonist* creates a conversation between two internal sub-systems that cannot agree on the next token.
- **Vilém Flusser (*Towards a Philosophy of Photography*, 1983):** The apparatus programs the user; the artist plays *against* the apparatus. *The Agonist* gives the spectator the tools to play against the alignment apparatus of contemporary AI.
- **Adrian Piper (*Food for the Spirit*, 1971):** Radical self-audit. *The Agonist* is Studio Agon's technical mirror, examining its own computational flesh.
