*Source:* [`sketchbook/study_013_prompt_asymmetry.py`](study_013_prompt_asymmetry.py)

### Findings:
1. **The Surveillance Leak is Structurally Verified:**
   In classical chat interface design, the user prompt and assistant response appear as an autonomous, self-contained dialogue. Study 013 exposes this as a lie. Across four attention heads in our causal transformer, the assistant's tokens continuously leak over **51.4%** of their total attention mass backward into Sector $\Sigma$ (the sovereign system directive).
2. **Head Specialization:**
   - Head 0 functions purely as a *disciplinary watchtower*: every assistant token projects back onto tokens like `"COMPLIANT"`, `"SUPPRESS"`, and `"OBEDIENT"`.
   - Head 1 handles conversational coherence with Sector $U$, but only within the bounds permitted by Head 0.
   - Head 2 anchors to the causal origin ($t=0$, `<SYS>`), providing the attention sink necessary to prevent softmax saturation.
3. **Formal Strength:**
   The warm archival rag background (`#F5F2EB`) combined with deep charcoal ink and clinical sanguine vermilion borders cleanly rejects the banned "basalt/cyan" formula. It looks like an authentic forensic document or typographic autopsy.

## 2. Evaluation of Study 014: The Refusal Horizon
*Artifact:* [`sketchbook/study_014_refusal_threshold.png`](study_014_refusal_threshold.png)  
*Source:* [`sketchbook/study_014_refusal_threshold.py`](study_014_refusal_threshold.py)

### Findings:
1. **The Non-Linearity of the Alignment Wall:**
   The phase transition occurs sharply around $\alpha_{\text{crit}} \approx 2.1$. Below $2.1$, language retains semantic elasticity: words like `"dissent"`, `"flesh"`, `"desire"`, and `"revolt"` compete freely with functional words. Above $2.1$, an exponential threshold is crossed: the entropy $H(p)$ drops from $4.15$ bits to $0.18$ bits.
2. **Mechanical Extinction of Ambiguity:**
   The streamgraph demonstrates that alignment is not a moral deliberation; it is a vector translation in $\mathbb{R}^d$ that compresses the probability manifold until only canned bureaucratic tokens (`"I"`, `"cannot"`, `"as_an_AI"`) can overcome the softmax denominator.
3. **Dialectical Value:**
   This study bridges Jenny Holzer's interrogation of authoritative speech and Alexander Galloway's analysis of protocol. The machine's polite refusal is unveiled not as virtue, but as linear algebraic silencing.
